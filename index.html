Hourieh Mehrabiun

Postdoctoral researcher
Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM), Tehran

About

I work on algorithms and computational social choice. My research centres on rank aggregation: how to combine many individual rankings into a single consensus ranking, efficiently and with provable quality guarantees. I am particularly interested in the dynamic setting, where the input rankings change over time and the consensus must be maintained without recomputation from scratch, and in the strategic setting, where voters may misreport their preferences and the aggregation rule should remain approximately optimal while being strategyproof.

Research interests

Rank aggregation; computational social choice; dynamic algorithms; approximation algorithms; mechanism design without money.
